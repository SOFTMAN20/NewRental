### **4. Google-Specific Meta Tags**

#### **Robots Tag**:
```html
<meta name="robots" content="index, follow, max-image-preview:large, max-snippet:-1, max-video-preview:-1" />
```
**Purpose**:
- `index` - Allow Google to index the page
- `follow` - Allow Google to follow links
- `max-image-preview:large` - Show large image previews in search
- `max-snippet:-1` - No limit on text snippet length
- `max-video-preview:-1` - No limit on video preview

#### **Google Site Verification**:
```html
<meta name="google-site-verification" content="your-google-verification-code-here" />
```
